04 adaptive learning
● you drive this one

It meets the student where they are — then moves.

Answer well and it climbs. Struggle and it doesn't punish you — it steps back and re-teaches. Try it: play the student.

You're at Level 2. The system is watching how you respond.
why not just harder

Difficulty is a lever, not a ladder to survive. When a learner slips, the system's first move is to find the missing idea and rebuild it — then raise the challenge again once it holds.

Quantum-inspired paths. At each step several routes are possible; the system narrows toward the one that fits this learner. It's a design metaphor for branching decisions — not a claim of quantum computing.

07 human × artificial

The AI never replaces the teacher. It hands them time.

Machines are good at the tireless work — noticing every mistake, generating endless practice, grading instantly. Teachers are good at the things that actually change a child.

The teacher brings

  • Motivation & mentorship
  • Emotional understanding
