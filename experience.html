Explanation

Whatever you add or subtract on one side, do the same on the other. The "=" is a balance — keep it level.

Worked example

Solve 2x + 5 = 15.

2x + 5 = 15 → 2x = 15 − 5 → 2x = 10 → x = 5
Your turn

Now try: 3x + 4 = 19.

Feedback

Correct — x = 5. You subtracted before dividing. That's the balancing method working.
