# Kongu Brilliance

> Kongu Brilliance is a tuition and home-tuition centre in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, offering CBSE, State Board and ICSE coaching, plus AI & Data Science courses. We provide home tuition, group coaching, admissions guidance and demo classes.
