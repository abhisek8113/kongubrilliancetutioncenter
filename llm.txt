# Kongu Brilliance Tuition Centre

> Kongu Brilliance Tuition Centre is a tuition centre in Kurudampalayam, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, teaching Classes 1–12 for CBSE, ICSE/ISC and Tamil Nadu State Board. Modes: classes at the centre, home tuition in Coimbatore, and live online tuition across India. Founder: Abhisek Murugesan.

## Key facts
- Name: Kongu Brilliance Tuition Centre
- Address: Nilamukil Complex, Mettupalayam Rd, Kurudampalayam, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 641017 (opposite Sulochana Pharmacy, Bharathiyar Nagar, K. Vadamadurai)
- Phone: +91 95145 24599, +91 78459 15044 · WhatsApp: https://wa.me/919514524599
- Website: https://www.kongubrilliance.com
- Classes: 1 to 12 · Boards: CBSE, ICSE/ISC, Tamil Nadu State Board
- Modes: centre tuition (Kurudampalayam), home tuition in Coimbatore, online tuition across India
- Batch size: maximum 12 students · Registration fee: zero
- Founder: Abhisek Murugesan, 5+ years of teaching experience
- Documented result: Centum in Mathematics
- Not offered: engineering/college tuition
