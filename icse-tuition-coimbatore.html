What makes ICSE different — and how we teach for it

Parents who move a child into ICSE, or from ICSE into Class 11 ISC, usually notice three things: the textbooks are denser, English carries more weight, and answers are expected to be complete and well presented rather than one-line facts. Our classes are planned around those three points.

Language-heavy papers

English Language and Literature are both examined in ICSE, and good English also helps in History, Geography and Biology answers. Students practise writing full answers, not just reading notes, and get them corrected.

Wide syllabus in Science and Maths

Physics, Chemistry and Biology are separate papers from the middle classes. We break each chapter into concepts, worked examples and practice questions, and revisit earlier chapters so nothing is left until the last month.

Presentation and working

In Mathematics and the sciences, marks depend on showing steps clearly. We train students to write working the way examiners expect, and check it in class.
