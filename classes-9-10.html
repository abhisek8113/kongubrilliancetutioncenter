How we prepare students for Class 10

Start in Class 9

Many Class 10 topics depend on Class 9 chapters. Students who join us in Class 9 get two full years to build the base.

Board-format practice

Students write answers for one-mark, short and long questions, and we correct them for content, steps and presentation — the things that decide marks.

Mathematics

Maths is where marks are most recoverable with practice. Centum in Mathematics is a documented result from our teaching; we work towards high scores through daily problem practice, not shortcuts.

Revision plan

From the last term we move to full-syllabus revision and timed papers, and track which chapters each student still finds hard.
