Why these years matter

Many students who struggle in Class 9 or 10 actually lost ground in Classes 6–8 — usually in algebra, fractions and decimals, or in learning how to write a full science answer. Middle school is the cheapest time to fix that.

Maths

Integers, fractions, ratio, basic algebra and geometry. We teach the idea first, then practise enough problems that the method sticks.

Science

Physics, chemistry and biology topics start to separate. Students learn to draw labelled diagrams and write definitions and explanations clearly.

English and study habits

Grammar, comprehension and composition — plus a simple weekly routine for revision so tests are not a last-minute rush.
