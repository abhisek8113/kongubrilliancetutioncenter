Frequently asked

Does the AI replace teachers?
No. The AI handles the repetitive work — setting papers, grading and spotting gaps — so our teachers can spend their time actually teaching and mentoring each student.
Which boards and classes are supported?
Papers and study material are aligned to CBSE and State board syllabi across school classes. Tell us your class and board and we'll tailor it.
How is grading fair and consistent?
The AI evaluates the method and key steps the same way for every student, every time — removing the variation you get with manual correction.
How do I get started?
Book a free demo or ask your admin for a login. You'll take a short diagnostic test, and your personalised plan is ready right after.
